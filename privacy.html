Agent Alerts Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Agent Alerts is a private automated notification service used to send low-volume system-status, market-monitoring, and ETF-monitoring alerts to opted-in recipients.

Information We Collect

We may collect a recipient's mobile phone number and basic messaging preferences solely for the purpose of delivering requested Agent Alerts notifications.

How We Use Information

Mobile numbers are used only to send requested Agent Alerts messages and related service communications.

Sharing of Mobile Information

Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt Out and Help

Reply STOP to opt out of future messages. Reply HELP for help.

Contact

For questions regarding Agent Alerts, contact the service owner using the contact information provided during enrollment.
