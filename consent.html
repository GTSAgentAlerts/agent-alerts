Agent Alerts SMS Consent Process

Agent Alerts is a private automated monitoring application operated by Gregory T. Scorsone.

Before a mobile number is enabled for Agent Alerts, the recipient is directly asked:

“Do you agree to receive automated Agent Alerts SMS notifications from Gregory Scorsone? Messages may include system-status, data-processing, market-monitoring, and ETF-monitoring notifications. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.”

Only recipients who explicitly agree are manually added to the application's SMS recipient list.

Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
